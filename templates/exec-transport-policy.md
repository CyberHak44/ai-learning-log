## 1. Purpose

To protect confidential information, including information that could move a share price or affect a regulatory matter, when executives travel. Vehicles now carry microphones, cameras and connected services. This policy treats a vehicle as a space that may be recording unless it has been checked.

## 2. Scope

Board members, C-level executives, [named senior roles], and anyone travelling on a sensitive matter (for example an acquisition, a regulatory hearing or a restructuring). It covers company cars, contracted transport, taxis and ride-hailing, in the UAE and abroad.

## 3. Transport tiers

| Tier | Transport | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Company fleet or approved executive transport vendor | Required for sensitive travel | Vehicle checked, in-cabin audio and voice assistants off, drivers under NDA |
| 2 | Pre-approved chauffeur service | Allowed with conditions | Booked through [travel portal]; confidentiality clause in contract |
| 3 | Taxi or ride-hailing | Not for sensitive matters | Allowed for routine travel; no sensitive conversations or calls |

## 4. Behaviour in transit

- Assume any vehicle outside tier 1 is recording.
- Do not discuss confidential or restricted information in tier 2 or 3 vehicles, or in public places.
- If a call cannot wait, use project code names, not real names.
- For urgent sensitive matters, type in [approved end-to-end encrypted messaging]. Do not use voice dictation in unchecked vehicles.

## 5. Company cars and fleet

- In-cabin audio recording, cabin cameras and voice assistants are switched off on fleet vehicles.
- A data protection impact assessment is completed before a new fleet model or connected feature is introduced.
- Vehicle data held by the manufacturer or telematics provider is covered by a data processing agreement: what is collected, where it is stored, who can access it, how long it is kept.
- Any stored audio or transcripts are deleted within [X] days.

## 6. Vehicle checks

- Tier 1 company vehicles: technical surveillance check every six months, and after any unsupervised servicing or valet parking.
- Vendor vehicles: vendor confirms in writing that in-cabin microphones and cameras are disabled; spot-checked by [Security].
- Signal jammers and similar devices must not be used. They are illegal under UAE telecommunications rules [confirm with TDRA].

## 7. Compliance

- [Security] reviews travel bookings every quarter.
- Breaches are handled under [disciplinary policy].
- Exceptions need written risk acceptance by [CISO].
