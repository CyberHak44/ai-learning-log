# AI as a force multiplier

Status: framework, 2026-11-03. Filled in as we go.

## The question

Where does AI genuinely let one person do the work of several, and where does it only look that way?

## Why it matters

For a founder-led firm, AI is the difference between one person and a small team. It only counts if the quality holds and the time saved is real.

## What we know so far

- In this portfolio, AI pair-building took grc-workbench from an empty repository to six working modules in a few days, with every stage committed by me.
- The multiplier is largest on first drafts, research and repetitive structure. It is smallest where judgement, accountability or client trust is the product.
