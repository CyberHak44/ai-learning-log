# AI governance frameworks

Status: round 1 started 2026-11-04. Filled in as we go.

## The question

Which frameworks should a business use to govern AI, and how do they fit with ISO/IEC 27001?

## Why it matters

Clients that already run an ISMS will be asked how they govern AI. The frameworks overlap with ISO 27001, so a GRC practitioner can extend what is already there.

## What we know so far

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the management system standard for AI, built on the same structure as ISO/IEC 27001.
- The NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 organises the work into four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
- EU AI Act: transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026; most high-risk obligations now apply from 2 December 2027.
- For UAE banks, CBUAE issued an AI guidance note in February 2026 covering governance, a model inventory, board accountability and third-party AI due diligence (secondary source; to confirm against CBUAE).
