# The AI tools: what each is for, and how I use them

Status: framework, 2026-11-03. Filled in as we go.

## The question

Polly (my AI assistant), Cursor, Grok, Gemini, Copilot: which tool for which job, and what happens to the data?

## Why it matters

Clients ask "which AI should we use?" The honest answer depends on the job and on where the data goes. Knowing the tools hands-on is part of advising on them.
